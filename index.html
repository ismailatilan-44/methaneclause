A speed camera for methane, written into the gas contract between an EU gas importer and its supplier.

Satellites confirm a leak → the seller's bond pays the buyer. No new leak on later passes → the seller earns money back in steps, up to the rebate, from a sponsor's pool (for example its lender's). No one runs the payout: once enough independent witnesses sign the same sighting and the appeal window passes, the contract pays the buyer from the bond. The seller can appeal — then the witnesses must confirm it again — but cannot delete the sighting or hold the money.

                                      Limits

                                      • Stake, not abatement. A rebate follows an attested plume; an earn-back follows passes that saw none. Annualised rates are a stake and only the stake's low bound is quoted. Observed, not abated.
                                      • Non-detection is not repair. A clean pass never cancels a rebate or counts as repair; it bounds how often a source emits above the instrument's floor, not its rate. It can release a refund that never exceeds the rebate that episode cost, is frozen while a new detection is under review, and is cancelled by any later attested detection at that source; installments already released are not clawed back, and the final share waits for the cure term.
                                      • Floor blindness, both ways. A source emitting just below the instrument's detection floor is invisible to the rebate and to the refund alike.
                                      • One provider's scene catalogue, and tasking. Qualified clean passes come from Carbon Mapper's catalogue; k-of-n witnesses guard against one witness's error or key, not against an error of the provider, and UNEP MARS is used against the negative (a MARS plume within the window refuses the pass). A seller can request Carbon Mapper passes during a planned shutdown, so the final tranche needs two instruments, the MARS corroboration and the cure term.
                                      • What the chain sees. It verifies that k organisations signed the same named fields; the cloud value itself is Carbon Mapper's. Witnesses attest what they received, from whom and when — not what the satellite saw; Carbon Mapper and UNEP do not sign their data.
                                      • Records only grow. Re-quantification never edits a confirmed episode (a later correction is a new record). Scenes published after the covenant's end plus the publication lag and the clean-pass window cannot be attested. A relapse is read from the core's latest episode at the source, so the sponsor watches and reports relapses.
                                      • Prototype keys and clocks. In this prototype the witness organisations are run by one person [SIM]; a judge's own wallet is designed to be the one signer we do not control. Seller, buyer and sponsor are [SIM]. The DZA replay runs on a [SIM clock]: every DZA date was public before this covenant was written, so the replay proves the mechanism, not the absence of foreknowledge. On Sepolia createdAt is real and later than the plumes and passes it settles (starts backdated); the chain shows the plume was public first. The satellites' sky positions and paths are drawn; their pass dates and images are real.
                                      • Post-quantum boundary. The on-chain check is secp256k1 ECDSA; ML-DSA-44 protects bundle authenticity off-chain. We do not claim a quantum-safe chain. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.
                                      • Not legal compliance, not a payment instrument. The exports are evidence for a regulator's form, never a finding of compliance. Money here is testnet ETH; where crypto payments are not lawful the verdict settles as a credit note.
                                      • Data licence. Carbon Mapper data is non-commercial; production needs a licence held by the witnesses.
