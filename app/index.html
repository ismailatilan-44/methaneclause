A speed camera for methane, written into the gas contract.

Satellites confirm a leak → the seller's bond pays the buyer. No new leak on later satellite passes → the seller earns money back in steps, never more than the rebate, from a sponsor's pool (for example its lender's). Once enough witnesses sign a sighting, no one — the seller included — can delete it or hold back the rebate on their own.

Public satellite records are the cameras; independent witnesses (separate organisations) each fetch the record and sign it; when enough of them sign the same leak and the appeal window passes, the seller's bond pays the buyer — the ticket goes to the buyer, not to a state. A clean pass never undoes a paid rebate or proves a repair; it only earns money back from the sponsor's pool.

                                    What this does not prove

                                    • Not abatement. A rebate follows an attested plume; an earn-back follows passes that saw none. Observed, not abated.
                                    • Not a repair verdict. A clean pass bounds how often a source emits above the instrument's floor — not its rate, not its repair.
                                    • Not legal compliance. The exports are evidence for a regulator's form, never a finding of compliance.
                                    • Not a payment instrument. Money here is testnet ETH; where crypto payments are not lawful the verdict settles as a credit note.
                                    • Not to scale. The satellites' sky positions and paths are drawn; their pass dates and images are real.

                                    Limits, said the same way everywhere

                                    • Non-detection is not repair. Non-detection never cancels a rebate or counts as repair. It can release a capped refund that the next attested detection cancels.
                                    • The refund is capped and cancellable. It never exceeds the rebate that episode cost, is frozen while a new detection is under review, and is cancelled by any later attested detection at that source. Installments already released are not clawed back; the final share waits for the cure term.
                                    • Floor blindness, both ways. A source emitting just below the instrument's detection floor is invisible to the rebate and to the refund alike.
                                    • One provider's scene catalogue. Qualified clean passes come from Carbon Mapper's catalogue. k-of-n witnesses guard against one witness's error or key, not against an error of the provider. UNEP MARS is used against the negative: a MARS plume within the window refuses the pass.
                                    • Tasking. A seller can request Carbon Mapper passes during a planned shutdown. That is why the final tranche needs two instruments, the MARS corroboration and the cure term.
                                    • Re-quantification never edits a confirmed episode. A later correction is a new record; the confirmed verdict stays.
                                    • Late scenes are lost. Scenes published after the covenant's end plus the publication lag and the window for qualified clean passes cannot be attested.
                                    • Relapses are reported, not discovered. A relapse is read from the core's latest episode at the source, so the sponsor watches and reports relapses.
                                    • The chain cannot see clouds. It verifies that k organisations signed the same named scene fields; the cloud value itself is Carbon Mapper's.
                                    • Witnesses attest what they received, from whom and when — not what the satellite saw. Carbon Mapper and UNEP do not sign their data.
                                    • Prototype keys. In this prototype the witness organisations are run by one person [SIM]; a judge's own wallet is designed to be the one signer we do not control. Seller, buyer and sponsor are [SIM].
                                    • The DZA replay runs on a [SIM clock]. Every DZA date was public before this covenant was written; the replay proves the mechanism, not the absence of foreknowledge. On Sepolia createdAt is real and later than the plumes and passes it settles (starts backdated); these runs prove the mechanism too, and the chain shows the plume was public first.
                                    • Post-quantum boundary. The on-chain check is secp256k1 ECDSA; ML-DSA-44 protects bundle authenticity off-chain. We do not claim a quantum-safe chain. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.
                                    • Stake, not abatement. Annualised rates are a stake. Only the stake's low bound is quoted. Money here is testnet ETH.
                                    • Data licence. Carbon Mapper data is non-commercial; production needs a licence held by the witnesses.
