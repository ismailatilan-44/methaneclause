# Model card — next-pass re-detection forecast

**Verdict: we measured it; the persistence rule is enough.** The gradient-boosted model fails the pre-registered
decision rule on the held-out 2026 set. Bonds are sized with the persistence rule in its smoothed form, because the
hard form forecasts exactly zero after a clean six months — the "absent ⇒ fixed" reading that is wrong 7–9 % of the
time on held-out data.

Every number below is regenerated by `make baseline model bond` and lives in
`data/derived/model/{splits,baseline_metrics,model_metrics,bond_sizing}.json` (audited in
`data/derived/audit_numbers.json`).

## Task

P(the next Carbon Mapper overpass of a source detects a methane plume | everything seen before that pass). Used for
one thing: **sizing the seller's bond** at covenant creation (and ranking the buyer's sources). No forecast ever
triggers, blocks or changes a payout; only k-of-n attested positive detections do.

Rules it had to beat, frozen before any training: **B1** = persistence ratio P = D/M over the trailing 6 months (the
ratio MARS uses); B2 = MARS rule "absent ⇒ 0". **B1s** = smoothed persistence (D + 0.45) / (M + 1.6), two constants fitted
on train by Brier (added at split v2, still a rule).

## Data and labels

Carbon Mapper per-source `observation_dates` / `detection_dates` (CH4, sector 1B2 oil & gas) for the ~2,000 sources
fetched around the MARS × Carbon Mapper join; label = the pass is a detection date; no hand labels. Licence: Carbon
Mapper Terms of Use (non-commercial, attribution, redistribution under the same terms) → derived fields only. Input hashes in `data/derived/model/splits.json`.
A "pass" is any date in Carbon Mapper's `observation_dates`. We do not apply Carbon Mapper's own qualified
null-detect definition (cloud and coverage thresholds; recorded in `data/derived/DATA_LICENSE.md` until `docs/DATA.md` exists), so some non-detection rows are cloudy or
partial scenes. That lowers the per-pass detection rate for every forecaster alike and is one more reason a clean pass
is not a clean site.

## Model

`HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, 15 features (trailing P, M, D; lifetime counts and ratio; days since last detection
and last pass; previous pass detected; log of the last detected rate; instrument of the next pass; |latitude|; season;
250 m vs 1000 m cluster), fixed hyperparameters, seed 0, no early stopping. `modelHash 0x141ccbbf…` (reproducible).

**Decision rule** (spec §5, operational in `train.py`): keep the model iff BSS vs B1 > 0 and ECE ≤ B1's on both
held-out sets. test_a passes, test_b fails on both conditions → **RULE_IS_ENOUGH**. Against B1s the model is worse on
both sets (BSS −0.035 and −0.111 [−0.233, −0.002]).

Breakdown (reported, not part of the decision): Algeria alone, model vs B1 −0.012 [−0.183, +0.141]; Turkmenistan alone
+0.065 [−0.026, +0.149]; test_b without Turkmenistan/Algeria −0.082 [−0.230, +0.050]. The model ranks passes well
(highest AUC on test_b) but is mis-calibrated under the 2026 shift, and it over-fits (train BSS +0.53).

## What we report but do not act on

- **Ablation without the next-pass instrument** would pass on these sets (BSS vs B1 +0.073 [+0.005, +0.132] and
  +0.157 [+0.091, +0.217]). We found that by looking at the test sets, so adopting it now would be choosing a model by
  its test score. A v2 model without the instrument feature has to be judged on data that arrives after today.
- The reviewer's post-hoc check on v1 also found a history-only logistic regression ahead of the boosted model. Same
  status: noted, not adopted.

## Bond sizing (the product path) and its sanity check

`bond.py`, as of the covenant start 2026-01-01: per facility, P(detection per pass) = max over its linked Carbon Mapper
sources (twin sources share most detections), passes per year from the trailing 12 months, payable share and tier mix
from history; 20,000 simulated years with at most one paid episode per month (≈ the 30-day linkWindow), per-pass
probability held constant over the year. **Recommended bond (P90 annual exposure, B1s): 0.015 ETH** (hard B1: 0.009;
model, not used: 0.029).

Backtest 2026-01-01 .. 2026-09-13, three facilities, a check of the bond logic and not a validation: the covenant would
really have paid **0.009 ETH** (TKM_S_276 four episodes, DZA_S_012 one, USA_S_077 none). B1s P10–P90 0.001–0.010
contains it; hard B1's P90 is 0.007 because it forecast DZA_S_012 (UNEP-confirmed mitigation, then a 694 kg/h plume on
2026-02-01) at exactly 0, where B1s said 0.17 per pass. All forecasters under-forecast TKM_S_276, whose pass rate rose
from 4/yr to 7 in eight months: tasking, not leaks, is the largest error source.

## Known failure modes

- Snapshot satellites miss intermittent plumes; "not detected on a pass" is not "not emitting" (detection floors:
  Tanager ~0.1 t/h, EMIT ~0.3 t/h).
- Carbon Mapper re-quantifies emissions and renames sources; labels move with provider re-processing (flagged by the
  audit diff in `make reproduce`, not hidden).
- Distribution shift by instrument and year; satellite pass rates change with tasking.
- Country comes from the nearest MARS source within 50 km; sources without one are "UNK".
