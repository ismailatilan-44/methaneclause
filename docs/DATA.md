# Data

Every input, where it comes from, under which terms, when it was taken and how to check it. Raw provider responses are
never committed (data/raw/ is ignored); [`data/MANIFEST.json`](../data/MANIFEST.json) keeps the SHA-256 of each one,
and every derived file in `data/derived/` records the hashes of its own inputs (`generatedFrom` / `inputs`).
Terms in full: [`data/derived/DATA_LICENSE.md`](../data/derived/DATA_LICENSE.md). Every number quoted anywhere is
regenerated by `make reproduce` (except the 15 Oct latency block, kept as recorded) and checked by `STRICT=1 make audit` against
[`data/derived/audit_numbers.json`](../data/derived/audit_numbers.json).

## Licence framing

This repository is a non-commercial research prototype: its contracts run on a local chain and on the Ethereum Sepolia
testnet with test tokens of no monetary value [SIM]; nothing is sold or paid. A production clause that settles real
payments on Carbon Mapper data would be commercial use and would need a commercial data agreement with Carbon Mapper,
held by the observers.

Carbon Mapper's terms are its own Terms of Use (effective 13 January 2026): non-commercial use (§3.2), attribution
"Data by Carbon Mapper®" (§3.3), redistribution under the same terms (§3.4), termination or suspension at any time
(§3.5). They are not a Creative Commons licence. "Share-alike" applies to UNEP IMEO MARS only (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Older
strings in `data/MANIFEST.json` and the earliest case files say "share-alike" for Carbon Mapper; the terms above apply.

## Data dependency and the path to production

The demo depends on two public datasets with non-commercial terms: Carbon Mapper (its own Terms of Use: non-commercial use, attribution, redistribution under the same terms) and UNEP IMEO MARS (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). A production clause that settles real payments on Carbon Mapper data needs a commercial data agreement with Carbon Mapper, held by the observer organisations; that agreement is planned if the clause is adopted. The design is not tied to one provider: every attestation names its provider and instrument (`providerId`, `instrumentId`), so an observer organisation can attest plumes from another source with the same contracts, such as NASA's EMIT methane plume product (EMIT L2B Estimated Methane Plume Complexes, doi:10.5067/EMIT/EMITL2BCH4PLM.001), which NASA releases as open data (CC0 unless marked otherwise; NASA data use guidance, read 2026-10-17). The observer service reads Carbon Mapper today; an EMIT reader is the step to production. UNEP MARS stays the corroborating provider (a refusal rule for every clean pass and a k-of-n corroboration for the final refund installment); a production clause using it this way asks UNEP for commercial use. The model clause carries the same terms (`docs/clause/model-clause.md`, clause 8).

## What is simulated [SIM]

- Seller, buyer, sponsor and surety are [SIM] parties; the importer labels ("EU importer", "Türkiye-type importer")
  are [SIM].
- In this prototype the observer organisations are run by one person [SIM]; the judge's own wallet is the one signer
  we do not control.
- Money is testnet ETH (Anvil and Sepolia) of no monetary value [SIM]; bond, bond sizes and fees are testnet-sized.
- Covenant start dates are backdated where the data is older than the covenant (recorded on chain as `createdAt`); the
  DZA replay runs on a pinned, forward-only local clock [SIM clock]. Every DZA date was public before that covenant was
  written: the replay proves the mechanism, not the absence of foreknowledge.
- The disagreement case in `make demo` and the cloudy-scene-signed-as-clear case are [SIM] edits of one field.
- The Annex C feedback forms are simulated seller claims [SIM].
- The portfolio covenant and the backtest are counterfactual: "if these sources had been under the clause; no clause
  existed" (`data/derived/backtest.json` `label`).

## What this data is not

A join candidate is a spatial-temporal match between two public datasets, not a confirmed attribution and not a
finding that MARS is wrong. A qualified clean pass is "observed, not abated". Non-detection is never evidence of repair.
Carbon Mapper re-processes plumes and can rename sources; values are pinned to the response hashes above, and
`make reproduce` reports a provider change as a provider change.
