# MethaneClause: a model methane clause for a gas or LNG supply agreement

**Draft for discussion — not legal advice.**

A buyer and a seller can adapt this text into a gas sale agreement (GSA) or an LNG sale and purchase agreement (SPA),
as a clause or a side letter. Square brackets mark choices the parties make. The values are the prototype's policy,
not a recommendation; the parties write their own and commit them in the same way. Every value below is rendered from
a committed file, never typed:

The clause has two sides: a **rebate** that an attested re-detection at a named source moves from the Seller's bond to
the Buyer, and a capped, cancellable **refund** that a run of qualified clean passes earns back for the Seller. Neither
side ever declares a source repaired. Schedule 2 names the contract function that enforces each clause.

## 1. Definitions

- **Covered Source**: a facility listed in the Annex to this clause; the list is committed as a Merkle root in the
  Covenant (`sourceSetRoot`), and a detection counts only with a proof that its source is on that list.
- **Covenant**: the on-chain instance of this clause, holding the Bond, the committed Policy (`policyHash`) and the
  Covered Sources.
- **Policy**: the parameters of this clause in canonical form (RFC 8785), committed by its SHA-256 hash before any
  evidence exists; Schedule 1 lists them.
- **Observer Organisation**: an organisation registered under clause 9 with its own signing keys, which is neither the
  Seller nor the Buyer and is not controlled by either.
- **Attested Detection**: a methane plume at a Covered Source, at or above the rate floor (`core.minRateKgH`), in a
  provider's published data, signed over the same canonical evidence bundle (EIP-712, its SHA-256 `evidenceHash`) by at
  least the Quorum.
- **Quorum**: `core.k` distinct Observer Organisations out of the `core.n` appointed.
- **Challenge Window**: the period (`core.challengeWindow`) after an Attested Detection is proposed during which anyone
  may challenge it under clause 5.
- **Tier**: the band of the attested emission rate that sets the Rebate (`core.rebateSchedule`).
- **Rebate**: the price adjustment due to the Buyer for a confirmed episode under clause 4.
- **Qualified Clean Pass**: a satellite scene of a Covered Source with a confirmed episode, after that episode's link
  window, that meets every scene rule of clause 6 and is signed by the Quorum; it records that a plume was not observed,
  and nothing more.
- **Refund Installment**: a share of the Refund cap released to the Seller under clause 6 after a stated number of
  counted Qualified Clean Passes.
- **Relapse**: a later Attested Detection at the same Covered Source after the claim's start that is not withdrawn by
  the Observer Organisations.
- **Sponsor Pool**: the fund, separate from the Bond, from which Refund Installments and observer fees are paid; it is
  opened by the Buyer or the Seller and may be topped up by a Sponsor (a lender, the Buyer or a programme).
- **Settlement Mode**: how money follows a verdict, chosen under clause 7: on chain (`ONCHAIN`) or by credit note
  under this agreement (`VERDICT_ONLY`).

## 2. The Seller's bond

**2.1** Before the start of the Term the Seller posts the Bond into the Covenant for the Covered Sources. [The Bond
amount is set by the bond-sizing rule committed with the Policy, at the [P90] of one year's rebate exposure.] The
Seller or any third party may top it up during the Term.

**2.2** The Bond may be posted by a surety. Payouts and returns are then assigned on chain to the surety, and the Seller
acts through it only to create the Covenant, contest an episode and record a mitigation statement.

**2.3** The Bond pays Rebates only. It returns to the Seller after the Term and the Challenge Window have ended and no
episode is open. Nobody, the parties included, can withdraw it earlier; there is no administrator.

## 3. The trigger

**3.1** Anyone may propose an episode by submitting, with the proposer's deposit, an Attested Detection at a Covered
Source together with the proof that the source is covered.

**3.2** An Observer Organisation of the Seller or the Buyer never counts towards the Quorum. Signatures that disagree on
the evidence produce no Quorum, and nothing happens.

**3.3** If no valid challenge is pending when the Challenge Window ends, anyone may finalize the episode as CONFIRMED.
A detection at the same source within the link window of a confirmed or pending episode is recorded as part of it and
carries no second Rebate.

**3.4** The clause acts only on positive evidence. No function accepts "no plume", and non-detection never cancels a
Rebate.

<!-- gen:trigger:begin -->
In the prototype policy: Quorum **k = 2 of n = 3** Observer Organisations (`core.k`, `core.n`); rate floor **100 kg/h** (`core.minRateKgH`), the super-emitter threshold of Art. 2(62) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1787 (`pool.euSuperEmitterKgH` = 100 kg/h); Challenge Window **1 day** (`core.challengeWindow`); link window **30 days** (`core.linkWindow`). The proposer's deposit is 0.0001 ETH [SIM] (`proposerBond`, fixed when the covenant contract is deployed; `contracts/deployments/11155111.json`).

## 4. Rebate tiers

**4.1** On CONFIRMED, the Rebate for the Tier of the attested rate is credited to the Buyer from the Bond (or recorded
for a credit note under clause 7), limited to the Bond remaining.

<!-- gen:tiers:begin -->
| Tier | Attested rate (`core.rebateSchedule.tierMinKgH`) | Rebate, settlement on chain (`core.rebateSchedule.tierAmountsWei`; testnet ETH in the prototype) | Rebate, credit note (`pool.unitTierAmounts`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 5,000 kg/h or more | 0.005 ETH [SIM] | 5.00 policy units |
| Tier 2 | 1,500 kg/h to below 5,000 kg/h | 0.003 ETH [SIM] | 3.00 policy units |
| Tier 3 | 100 kg/h to below 1,500 kg/h | 0.001 ETH [SIM] | 1.00 policy units |
<!-- gen:tiers:end -->

**4.2** The Rebate is a price adjustment under this agreement for gas from a Covered Source during an attested
episode. [The parties state whether it is the Buyer's sole remedy for that episode.]

## 5. Challenge and resolution

**5.1** Anyone may challenge a proposed episode within the Challenge Window, with the challenge deposit, on one of the
objective, re-checkable reasons: the evidence hash does not match the provider's data; the source is not covered; the
plume was already used; a signing key was revoked; the data is stale.

**5.2** The Observer Organisations answer a challenge by the Quorum: confirmation makes the episode CONFIRMED and the
challenger's deposit goes to the Buyer; withdrawal makes it VOID, the proposer's deposit goes to the challenger and
the Bond is untouched.

**5.3** The Seller may contest attribution (a scientific dispute, not an objective error) within the Challenge Window.
A contest holds the Rebate and slashes nobody; it is confirmed only by one Observer Organisation more than the Quorum.

**5.4** A challenge or contest left unanswered expires: the held Rebate returns to the Bond and nobody is slashed.

<!-- gen:challenge:begin -->
In the prototype policy: a challenge deposit of 0.0002 ETH [SIM] (`challengerBond`, fixed at deployment); a resolution needs 2 Observer Organisations, and 3 to confirm over a contest; the contest window is **2 days** (`core.contestWindow`).
<!-- gen:challenge:end -->

## 6. The refund side

**6.1** When an episode is CONFIRMED, anyone may open a refund claim on the Sponsor Pool. The claim's cap is the Rebate
that episode cost; the Refund never exceeds it.

**6.2** A Qualified Clean Pass is a scene after the claim's start that the provider flags as not cloudy, whose footprint
covers the source's plume origins, that shows no plume at the source, with no UNEP MARS plume near the source within the
stated window, and taken by an instrument whose detection floor is at most the episode's rate. The Quorum signs the
scene record; anyone may challenge it within its clean window on one of the matching objective reasons.

**6.3** Counted passes schedule the Refund Installments below. The final installment also needs passes from two
instruments, a MARS corroboration signed by the Quorum, and the Cure Term.

<!-- gen:refund:begin -->
In the prototype policy: a pass counts after a clean window of **1 day** (`pool.cleanWindow`) and no earlier than **60 days** after the scene (`pool.publicationLag`); a challenged pass is answered within 7 days (`pool.resolveWindow`); a scheduled installment is released no earlier than **64 days** after scheduling (`pool.holdWindow`). Pass deposit 0.0001 ETH, challenge deposit 0.0002 ETH, observer fee 0.00001 ETH per counted pass, shared by the signing organisations (all [SIM], `pool.passBondWei`, `pool.challengerBondWei`, `pool.observerFeeWei`). Scene rules: Carbon Mapper's `not_cloudy` flag (`pool.cloudRule`), consistent with a cloud cover of at most 25 % (`pool.maxCloudBp`); no UNEP MARS plume within ±7 days (`pool.marsWindowDays`); the instrument's detection floor (EMIT 700 kg/h, Tanager 180 kg/h; `pool.instrumentFloorKgH`) at most the episode's rate.

| Counted passes (`pool.installments`) | Installment | Also needs |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 30 % of the cap | — |
| 6 | 30 % of the cap | — |
| 10 | 40 % of the cap | passes from two instruments; a MARS corroboration covering its triggering pass; the Cure Term of 540 days (`pool.cureTerm`) plus the relapse grace of 63 days (`pool.publicationLag` + `core.challengeWindow` + `core.contestWindow`) since the claim's start |
<!-- gen:refund:end -->

**6.4** While a new detection at the source is under review, no pass is attested or counted and no installment is
released. A Relapse cancels the claim: every installment not yet released is void. Installments already released stay
released.

**6.5** Each counted pass pays the observer fee from the Sponsor Pool to the signing Observer Organisations.
## 7. Settlement by jurisdiction

**7.1** The verdict (CONFIRMED, VOID, counted, cancelled) is the same in every Settlement Mode. Only the way money
follows it differs.

**7.2** **On chain** (`ONCHAIN`), where the parties may lawfully settle in a digital asset: the Bond and the Sponsor
Pool hold the settlement asset and Rebates and Refunds are paid out of them. [In the EU the parties may denominate it in
a euro e-money token under MiCA, which is electronic money redeemable at par.] The prototype uses testnet ETH.

**7.3** **By credit note** (`VERDICT_ONLY`), where a party may not use crypto-assets in payments (for example a
Türkiye-type importer under the CBRT regulation on crypto-assets in payments): no asset is held on chain; the Covenant
records the verdict and the amount in policy units, and the party owing issues a credit note or bank transfer under
this agreement that cites the verdict reference. The credit note is not a payment instrument.

**7.4** Payouts are pulls: no contract pushes money to a party. Checks on paying a party in a third country (AML, KYC,
sanctions) stay with the paying party and the Sponsor, outside the Covenant.

## 8. Data sources and licences

**8.1** Detections and clean passes are read from public satellite data providers. The prototype uses Carbon Mapper
("Data by Carbon Mapper®", under Carbon Mapper's own non-commercial Terms of Use) and UNEP IMEO MARS
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) as the corroborating provider. Settling real payments on these data is commercial use: each Observer
Organisation that signs on Carbon Mapper data holds a commercial data agreement with Carbon Mapper [and a permission
from UNEP for MARS], before the Term starts.

**8.2** Each attestation names its provider and instrument, so the parties may list other providers in the Annex
(for example NASA's EMIT methane plume product, released as NASA open data). The path to production is set out in
[`docs/DATA.md`](../DATA.md#data-dependency-and-the-path-to-production).

**8.3** Raw provider data stays off chain with the providers and the Observer Organisations; the Covenant holds only
hashes, identifiers and money. Each Observer Organisation keeps the provider response it signed for the life of the
Covenant [plus the limitation period].

## 9. Observer appointment and replacement

**9.1** The parties appoint the Observer Organisations named in the Annex at signing [at least one licensed
satellite-data provider, one academic group and one independent analyst]. Each registers its own keys in the registry
the Covenant trusts, under its own controller key.

**9.2** An Observer Organisation may rotate its keys at any time and must revoke a key it no longer controls; a
signature from a revoked key does not count, and a revoked key is a reason to challenge.

**9.3** If an Observer Organisation resigns, loses its data access or is shown to be controlled by a party, the
remaining Observer Organisations admit a replacement from a reserve list agreed at signing, by majority vote after an
admission delay. [The prototype runs this governance registry on a local chain only.]

**9.4** Observer Organisations are paid the observer fee for counted passes and [an annual fee from the Sponsor]. No
Observer Organisation is paid by the Seller or the Buyer alone.

## 10. Term and termination

**10.1** The Covenant runs for the Term from its start. After the Term, episodes already proposed run to the end of
their windows; the Sponsor Pool accepts passes for a further publication period and then closes.

<!-- gen:term:begin -->
In the prototype policy: Term **365 days** (`core.term`) from 2026-01-01 (`core.start`); the Sponsor Pool stops accepting passes 61 days after the Term ends (`pool.publicationLag` + `pool.cleanWindow`), because scenes are published late. The final installment (clause 6) is scheduled only for an episode at least **573 days** before the Term ends (`core.linkWindow` + `pool.cureTerm` + the relapse grace − `pool.publicationLag`); with this 365-day Term it is never scheduled and a refund stops at 60 % of the cap. Drafting note: set the Term to the supply contract's own (multi-year); the final share then stays reachable for every episode up to 573 days before the Term ends (`POLICY_V2_MULTIYEAR`: Term 1,095 days, every episode in its first 522 days; on Ethereum Sepolia as covenant 2, its pool opened under this policy).
<!-- gen:term:end -->

**10.2** Termination of the supply agreement ends the Term for the Covered Sources it covers; it does not void a
verdict, a pending episode or a scheduled installment, each of which reaches a final state through a call anyone can
make.

**10.3** On closing, the unreserved balance of the Sponsor Pool returns to its contributors pro rata, and the
remaining Bond returns to the Seller.

## 11. No admission of liability

A CONFIRMED episode, a Rebate or a credit note under this clause is a price adjustment agreed in advance. It is not an
admission by the Seller of fault, liability or breach of any law. The Seller may record its own statement on chain
(a mitigation statement, logged by its hash; it changes no state). [Nothing in this clause limits a party's other
remedies, unless the parties so agree in clause 4.2.]

## 12. What the clause records

This clause records verdicts and does not certify emission reductions. It records that the Quorum attested a detection
or a clean pass at a Covered Source and what money followed. A Qualified Clean Pass is observed, not abated, and a
Refund is an incentive under this agreement, not a finding of repair. The clause does not measure a facility's total
emissions, does not declare a source repaired, and is not a document of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/1787. Its records (IEA
Annex D export) may be attached as evidence to the reports that Regulation asks for, and they complement the
information clauses of Recommendation (EU) 2026/1834, which contain no remedy term.
